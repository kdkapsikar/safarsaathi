# Safar Saathi help

Each section is one help topic for the website assistant (`site_help` tool). Keep answers short and true to how the site works today.

## What is Safar Saathi

keywords: about, what, who, safar saathi, app, service, free
Safar Saathi watches your Indian Railways train and alerts you, and later the people waiting for you, about delays, platform changes, departures, arrivals, cancellations and diversions. It's free and has no ads.

## Creating an account

keywords: sign up, signup, register, account, create account, join
Choose "Get started" or "Create a free account" on the home page and enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. You stay signed in on that browser until you sign out.

## Signing in and out

keywords: sign in, login, log in, sign out, logout, password, forgot
Use "Sign in" with your email and password. "Sign out" is at the top right of the dashboard. There's no password reset yet.

## Adding a journey

keywords: add, journey, track, create, new, train, watch, alert me
On the dashboard, fill in "Add a journey": train number (5 digits), from and to station codes (start typing a name to search), the date you board, and which alerts you want. You can also ask me to add one; I'll show a card for you to confirm first.

## Alert types

keywords: alert, alerts, notification, types, departure, arrival, delay, platform, what alerts
Departure: when the train leaves your station. Arrival: about 30 minutes before it reaches your destination. Delay: when it's at least 15 minutes late (or your own threshold), then again at 30, 60, 90 minutes and so on. Platform change: when the platform at your station is announced or changes. Cancellations and diversions are always sent.

## Where alerts appear

keywords: bell, where, see, inbox, notifications, email, sms, whatsapp
Alerts appear behind the bell icon on your dashboard. Email delivery is being set up and isn't live yet. SMS and WhatsApp are planned.

## Quiet hours and delay thresholds

keywords: quiet, night, sleep, threshold, minutes, rules, smart rules, settings
Each alert type can have quiet hours, when non-urgent alerts are held back until they end, and the delay alert can have its own minimum delay. Cancellations and diversions ignore quiet hours. Screens to change these are coming soon.

## Deleting a journey

keywords: delete, remove, cancel journey, stop tracking
Use the bin icon on the journey card and confirm. You can also ask me to delete one; I'll show a card for you to confirm first.

## Where the train data comes from

keywords: data, source, accurate, live, ntes, irctc, simulated, where does, how do you know
Live running data comes from a train-data source. During this early preview it's a simulator, so times and delays are simulated, not real. Every answer shows the time the data was checked ("as of").

## Privacy

keywords: privacy, data, personal, share, delete account, security
We store your name, email, password (hashed) and the journeys you add. We don't sell data or show ads. Account deletion isn't self-service yet.
